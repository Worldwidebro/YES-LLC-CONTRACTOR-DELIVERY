## Contingencies

**IF budget not approved by Week 1:**
- Reduce to Services 2, 4 = $8-10K
- Defer 1, 3, 5, 6, 7 to Phase 2

**IF team leads unavailable:**
- Use contractors + internal staff
- Extend timeline 2 weeks
- Budget +$5-8K

**IF tool licenses delayed:**
- Use open-source (OWASP ZAP, SonarQube)
- Delay Service 1 start 1 week
- Integrate Week 3 vs. Week 2
